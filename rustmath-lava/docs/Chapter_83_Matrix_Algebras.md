**Handbook part:** XI — Algebras
**Handbook pages:** 2509–2544 (PDF pages 2638–2677)

## Scope and overview

Chapter 83 covers matrix algebras (also called matrix rings) defined over an arbitrary ring S.
The complete n × n matrix algebra over S is denoted Mn(S) and is alternatively regarded as the
endomorphism ring of the free S-module S(n) (the "natural S-module" associated with Mn(S)).
Matrix algebras have type `AlgMat` and their elements have type `AlgMatElt`; both inherit from
`AlgMatV` / `AlgMatVElt`, which also subsume matrix Lie algebras.

The chapter progresses from basic construction and element arithmetic through canonical forms
(Echelon, Hermite, Smith, Rational, Jordan) to a substantial algorithmic section (§83.14) that
computes presentations for algebras over finite fields. The presentation algorithm is built
around the Jacobson radical: the short exact sequence 0 → Rad(A) → A → A/Rad(A) → 0 is
split, A/Rad(A) decomposes as a direct sum of complete matrix algebras over extensions Ki of
the base field, and generators for the radical together with field generators and permutation
matrices yield a free-algebra presentation A ≅ P/I. The Cartan matrix is computed as a
by-product.

The determinant algorithm over Z and Q is the modular method of Abbott–Bronstein–Mulders
**[ABM99]** combined with a fast p-adic divisor step; the characteristic polynomial has four
selectable algorithms (Modular, Hessenberg, Interpolation, Trace). Section 83.12 diagonalises
commutative algebras by computing common eigenspaces over splitting fields of the base field.

## 83.1 Introduction

Matrix algebras may be defined over any ring S. The complete algebra Mn(S) is the endomorphism
ring of the free S-module S(n). Types: `AlgMat` (algebras), `AlgMatElt` (elements). Both types
inherit from `AlgMatV` / `AlgMatVElt`.

## 83.14 Presentations for Matrix Algebras

Magma can compute a presentation A ≅ P/I for a matrix algebra A over a finite field k generated
by matrices α₁, …, αₜ, where P is a free associative algebra in noncommuting variables and I is
a two-sided ideal. The algorithm exploits the split short exact sequence
0 → Rad(A) → A → A/Rad(A) → 0: A/Rad(A) decomposes as a direct sum of complete matrix
algebras Aᵢ over extensions Kᵢ of k; each Aᵢ is generated by a field generator bᵢ (minimal
rank, bᵢⁿⁱ⁻¹ = eᵢ a primitive idempotent, generating the multiplicative group of eᵢAeᵢ ≅ Kᵢ)
and a permutation matrix tᵢ of degree nᵢ. Radical generators z₁, …, zₛ are chosen in the
condensed algebra eAe (e = Σeᵢ). The condensed algebra eAe is Morita equivalent to A and is
computed alongside the presentation.

Returns a sequence of records (one per simple quotient), each with fields: `AlgebraIdempotent`, `PrimitiveIdempotent`, `PrimitiveIdempotentOnQuotient`, `FieldGenerator`, `FieldGeneratorOnQuotient`, `GeneratingPolForCenter`. | Idempotent lifting from semisimple quotient |
| `PrimitiveIdempotents(A)` | A list of primitive idempotents for A, one for each irreducible module. | — |
| `RanksOfPrimitiveIdempotents(A)` | The sequence of ranks of primitive idempotents of A. | — |
| `CondensedAlgebra(A)` | The algebra eAe where e is a sum of primitive idempotents, one for each simple A-module. Morita equivalent to A, and much smaller in dimension. | Condensation (MeatAxe-style) |
| `CartanMatrix(A)` | The Cartan matrix of A: the matrix of multiplicities of composition factors. | Radical/idempotent structure |

*Worked example: H83E10 (block upper-triangular algebra over GF(3): simple quotients, primitive idempotents, Cartan matrix); H83E11 (permutation module of Sym(7) over GF(5): 7 simple quotients, condensed algebra, Cartan matrix showing 4 blocks).*

### 83.14.2 Generators and Presentations

Variables in the free algebra presentation are named: b.i for field generators, t.i for
permutation matrices, z.i for radical generators.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `SemisimpleGeneratorData(A)` | Data on the semisimple generators of A (i.e. generators of A/Rad(A)). Returns a sequence of records (one per simple quotient) with fields: `PowersOfFieldGenerators`, `Permutation`, `PermutationOnQuotient`, `FieldGenerator`, `FieldGeneratorOnQuotient`, `PrimitiveIdempotent`, `PrimitiveIdempotentOnQuotient`, `GeneratingPolForCenter`. | Semisimple (Wedderburn) component extraction |
| `AlgebraGenerators(A)` | The standard generators of A. Returns a record with fields: `FieldGenerators`, `PermutationMatrices`, `PrimitiveIdempotents`, `RadicalGenerators` (list of lists for each eᵢAeⱼ), `SequenceRadicalGenerators`, `GeneratingPolynomialsForCenters`, `StandardFormConjugationMatrices`. | Radical generators + field/permutation generators |
| `AlgebraStructure(A)` | The accumulated structure of A. Returns a record with fields: `FreeAlgebra`, `RelationsIdeal`, `StandardFreeAlgebraCover`, `FieldGenerators`, `PermutationMatrices`, `PrimitiveIdempotents`, `RadicalGenerators`, `CondensedRadicalBasis` (list of lists of (matrix, monomial) tuples), `CondensedFieldGenerators`, `FieldPolynomials`, `DegreesOfSimpleModules`, `DegreeOfFieldExtensions`, `SimpleQuotientAlgebras`, `StandardFormConjugationMatrices`. | Full structure computation: radical, idempotents, presentations |
| `Presentation(A)` | The presentation A ≅ P/I in generators and relations. Returns the free algebra P, the relations ideal I, and the map P → A. | Non-commutative Gröbner basis for radical structure |
| `StandardFormConjugationMatrices(A)` | The pair (M, M⁻¹) of conjugation matrices putting A into standard form relative to its primitive idempotents. | — |
| `CondensationMatrices(A)` | The matrices that implement the condensation of A to eAe. | — |
| `SequenceOfRadicalGenerators(A)` | The sequence of matrices generating Rad(A). | Radical generators (module structure) |
| `CartanMatrix(A)` | The Cartan matrix of A (also computable directly from §83.14.1). | — |

### 83.14.3 Solving the Word Problem

The presentation machinery also provides membership testing: given a subalgebra A of Mn(k)
generated by matrices, Magma can decide if any n × n matrix belongs to A and, if so, express
it as a polynomial in the presentation generators.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `WordProblemData(A)` | The data needed to solve the word problem for A: a list of lists of basis elements for the radical together with corresponding monomials in the free algebra generators. | — |
| `WordProblem(A, x)` | True iff matrix x ∈ A; if true, also returns an expression of x as a polynomial in the presentation of A. | Non-commutative normal form via Gröbner basis |

*Worked example: H83E12 (permutation module of Sym(5) over GF(2), dim = 42; Presentation, CartanMatrix, CondensedAlgebra, WordProblem).*
