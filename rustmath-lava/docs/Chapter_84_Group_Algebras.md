**Handbook part:** XI — Algebras

## Scope and overview

Group algebras (also called group rings) may be defined over any unital ring R and any group G
in which elements have a canonical form — for example permutation groups, matrix groups, or
polycyclic groups. Basic arithmetic on elements is available in any such group algebra. Certain
functions place stricter requirements: for subalgebra and ideal computations, R must possess a
matrix echelon algorithm in Magma; quotient algebras additionally require R to be a field.

Two internal representations of group algebra elements are provided. The **vector representation**
fixes an ordering (g₁, g₂, …, gₙ) of all elements of G (requiring |G| to be finite and not too
large) and stores each algebra element as a coefficient vector of length n = |G|. This enables
fast arithmetic and matrix echelonisation for subalgebras and ideals. The **terms representation**
stores each element as a pair of parallel arrays (nonzero coefficients and their associated group
elements), allowing group algebras over any group with canonical forms — including potentially
infinite matrix groups over characteristic-0 rings or free groups — at the cost of arrays that
may grow exponentially under repeated multiplication. Magma selects the vector representation
when |G| ≤ 1000 and the terms representation otherwise, unless overridden by the user.

Subalgebras and ideals of a group algebra are not themselves group algebras, so a separate type
`AlgGrpSub` is used for them. Alternatively they may be coerced into associative algebras with
structure constants (type `AlgAss`).

## 84.2 Construction of Group Algebras and their Elements

### 84.2.1 Construction of a Group Algebra

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `GroupAlgebra(R, G: parameters)` / `GroupAlgebra< R, G: parameters >` | Given a unital ring R and a group G (not a finitely presented group), create the group algebra R[G]. Optional parameter `Rep` (MonStgElt, default: Magma-chosen) selects the element representation: `"Vector"` (requires computing \|G\|; errors if too large) or `"Terms"` (sparse parallel-array form). Optional parameter `Table` (BoolElt, default: Magma-chosen) controls whether the multiplication table of G is precomputed and stored; only possible with the vector representation; stored by default when \|G\| ≤ 200. | — |

*Worked examples: H84E1 (default group algebra over Z for S₃; group algebra over GF(5) for the dihedral group of order 100 as a polycyclic group, using terms representation).*

### 84.2.2 Construction of a Group Algebra Element

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `elt< A \| r, g >` | Given group algebra A = R[G], ring element r ∈ R, and group element g ∈ G, create the element r∗g of A. | — |
| `A ! g` | Given a group element g ∈ G, create the element 1_R∗g in A = R[G]. | — |
| `A ! r` | Given a ring element r ∈ R, create the element r∗1_G in A = R[G]. | — |
| `A ! [c1, ..., cn]` | Given A in vector representation and a sequence [c₁, …, cₙ] of n = \|G\| elements of R, create the element c₁∗g₁ + … + cₙ∗gₙ, where (g₁, …, gₙ) is the fixed basis of A. | — |
| `Eta(A)` | For A = R[G] in vector representation, create the element Σ_{g∈G} 1_R∗g of A. | — |

*Worked examples: H84E2 (verifying that Eta(A)/\|G\| is idempotent in GF(7)[Alt(6)]).*

## 84.3 Construction of Subalgebras, Ideals and Quotient Algebras

This section applies when A = R[G] is in vector representation and R is a unital ring with a
matrix echelon algorithm. Subalgebras and ideals are of type `AlgGrpSub` (default) or `AlgAss`
(structure-constant algebra); both constructions also return the inclusion homomorphism into A.
Quotient algebras require R to be a field and are returned as associative algebras.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `sub< cat: A \| L >` | Create the subalgebra S of group algebra A generated by elements defined by list L. L may contain elements of A, sets/sequences of elements of A, subalgebras of A, sets/sequences of subalgebras, or sequences of ring elements. Optional `cat` parameter (Cat, default: `AlgGrpSub`) selects the output category (`AlgGrpSub` or `AlgAss`). Returns S and the inclusion homomorphism f : S → A. | — |
| `lideal< cat: A \| L >` | Create the left ideal of A generated by L (same list format as `sub`). Optional `cat` (default: `AlgGrpSub`). Returns the ideal and inclusion f : I → A. | — |
| `rideal< cat: A \| L >` | Create the right ideal of A generated by L. Optional `cat` (default: `AlgGrpSub`). Returns the ideal and inclusion f : I → A. | — |
| `ideal< cat: A \| L >` | Create the two-sided ideal I of A generated by L. Optional `cat` (default: `AlgGrpSub`). Returns I and inclusion f : I → A. The above four constructors may also take a subalgebra S (type `AlgGrpSub`) in place of A, computing closures within S. | — |
| `a * I` | For a right ideal I of A, construct the right ideal {a∗b : b ∈ I}. | — |
| `I * a` | For a left ideal I of A, construct the left ideal {b∗a : b ∈ I}. | — |
| `quo< A \| L >` | Create the quotient of A by the ideal generated by L. Returns the quotient Q (as an associative algebra) and the natural homomorphism f : A → Q. | — |
| `A / S` | The quotient of A by the ideal closure of its subalgebra S. | — |

*Worked examples: H84E3 (Wedderburn decomposition of GF(4)[G] for an extraspecial group of order 3³ via `MinimalIdeals`; character degrees recovered as square roots of component dimensions).*

## 84.4 Operations on Group Algebras and their Subalgebras

### 84.4.1 Operations on Group Algebras

These operations apply only to a full group algebra (not subalgebras), unless noted.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `Algebra(A)` | For A in vector representation, construct the associative structure-constant algebra B isomorphic to A, together with the isomorphism A → B. | — |
| `AugmentationMap(A)` | The augmentation map of A: the ring homomorphism A → R sending Σ_{g∈G} r_g∗g to Σ_{g∈G} r_g. | — |
| `AugmentationIdeal(A)` | The augmentation ideal of A (in vector representation): the kernel of the augmentation map. | — |
| `RepresentationType(A)` | Returns `"Vector"` or `"Terms"` depending on which representation is used for elements of A. | — |
| `ChangeRepresentationType(A, Rep)` | Construct an isomorphic group algebra B with elements represented as specified by Rep (`"Vector"` or `"Terms"`), together with the homomorphism A → B. | — |
| `ConstructTable(A)` | Procedure: for A = R[G] in vector representation, construct and store the multiplication table for G to speed up multiplication. Does nothing if the table already exists. | — |
| `CoefficientRing(A)` / `BaseRing(A)` | The coefficient ring (base ring) of A. | — |

### 84.4.2 Operations on Subalgebras of Group Algebras

These functions can be applied to a full group algebra A or to subalgebras of type `AlgGrpSub`.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `S ! 1` | Create the identity element of the group algebra (or subalgebra) S. For a proper subalgebra this may differ from the identity of the full group algebra. | — |
| `Group(S)` | The group G for the group algebra or subalgebra S. | — |
| `GroupAlgebra(S)` | The group algebra of which S is a subalgebra. | — |
| `Module(S)` | For a subalgebra S of A = R[G], return the submodule of the module underlying A corresponding to S — an R-module of dimension Dimension(S) and degree Dimension(A). Also returns the natural map from the subalgebra to the module. | — |
| `CoefficientRing(A)` / `BaseRing(A)` | The coefficient ring (base ring) of A (or subalgebra). | — |
| `BasisMatrix(S)` | For a subalgebra S of A = R[G], return the m × \|G\| coefficient matrix of the basis of S with respect to the basis of A, where m = Dimension(S). | — |
| `Coordinates(S, a)` | Given an element a lying in subalgebra S, return a sequence giving the coordinates of a with respect to the basis of S. | — |
| `IsLeftIdeal(S)` | Returns true if S is a left ideal of its group algebra; otherwise false. | — |
| `IsRightIdeal(S)` | Returns true if S is a right ideal of its group algebra; otherwise false. | — |
| `IsIdeal(S)` | Returns true if S is a two-sided ideal of its group algebra; otherwise false. | — |
| `Centraliser(S)` / `Centralizer(S)` | The centralizer of subalgebra S in the group algebra A. | — |
| `Idealiser(S)` / `Idealizer(S)` | The largest subalgebra T of A such that S is an ideal in T. | — |
| `LeftAnnihilator(S)` | The left annihilator of S in A: the subalgebra of all a ∈ A such that a∗s = 0 for all s ∈ S. | — |
| `RightAnnihilator(S)` | The right annihilator of S in A: the subalgebra of all a ∈ A such that s∗a = 0 for all s ∈ S. | — |

*Worked examples: H84E4 (Jacobson radical of GF(2)[Z₂⁵] via `JacobsonRadical`; nilpotency verified by iterated ideal product; nilpotency class 6 determined from sequence of dimensions).*

## 84.5 Operations on Elements

These operations apply to elements of either a full group algebra or a group algebra subalgebra
of type `AlgGrpSub`. Only operations additional to those available for general algebras are listed.

| Intrinsic | Description | Algorithm |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| `a + r` / `r + a` | Sum of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and scalar r ∈ R. | — |
| `a + g` / `g + a` | Sum of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and group element g ∈ G. | — |
| `a - r` / `r - a` | Difference of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and scalar r ∈ R. | — |
| `a - g` / `g - a` | Difference of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and group element g ∈ G. | — |
| `a * r` / `r * a` | Product of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and scalar r ∈ R. | — |
| `g * a` / `a * g` | Product of group algebra element a ∈ R[G] and group element g ∈ G. | — |
| `Support(a)` | The support of a: the sequence of group elements whose coefficients in a are nonzero. | — |
| `Trace(a)` | The trace of a: the coefficient of 1_G in a. | — |
| `Augmentation(a)` | The augmentation of a: Σ_{g∈G} r_g where a = Σ_{g∈G} r_g∗g. | — |
| `Involution(a)` | If a = Σ_{g∈G} r_g∗g, returns Σ_{g∈G} r_g∗g⁻¹. | — |
| `Coefficient(a, g)` / `a[g]` | The coefficient of g ∈ G in a ∈ R[G]. | — |
| `ElementToSequence(a)` / `Eltseq(a)` | For A in vector representation: sequence of coefficients with respect to the fixed basis of A. For A in terms representation: sequence of tuples (coefficient, group element) for each nonzero term. | — |
| `Coefficients(a)` | For A in vector representation, returns the sequence of coefficients of a with respect to the fixed basis of A. | — |
| `Centraliser(a)` / `Centralizer(a)` | The centralizer of element a in the group algebra A. | — |
| `Centraliser(S, a)` / `Centralizer(S, a)` | The centralizer of element a (of A) in the subalgebra S of A. | — |

*Worked examples: H84E5 (diameter of the Cayley graph of Alt(6) via word counting in group algebra Q[Alt(6)]; `Support`, `Parent`, `Group`, element arithmetic); H84E6 (convergence of random walks on M₁₁ towards uniform distribution, measured via sum of squared coefficient differences in R[16][M₁₁]; `Eta`, `Eltseq`).*
