## Method

The 45 chapters were split into 73 chunks of at most about 100 rows, cut at section boundaries.
Each chunk went through three agents:

1. A **reviewer** (Sonnet). It read the chapter digest and searched the home crate, and then the
   whole workspace, by meaning. It read each function and its tests and wrote
   `id,status,rust_path,note` for every row.
2. An **independent verifier**. It checked every row the reviewer did not mark `missing`, against
   the code and the cited test (the test must exist, not be `#[ignore]`d, and exercise the function).
   It also checked at least 30% of the `missing` rows, and all of them if its sample found two or more
   wrong. That escalation happened in 51 of the 73 chunks.
3. An **adjudicator**, for every row where the two disagreed. It read the code itself and decided.

The verifier checked 4,685 rows, 95% of all rows, and disagreed on 699 of them (15%). The
adjudicator sided with the verifier 666 times and with the reviewer 14 times, and chose a third
status 19 times. The reviewer's first pass was therefore wrong on about one row in seven. Most of
its errors were missed implementations under other names and statuses that were too generous.

Two mechanical checks then ran over all reviewed rows, including the pilot chapters:

- **Code that never compiles.** The dep-info files from the full workspace build
  (`target/debug/deps/*.d`) list every source file that was compiled. These files are not among
  them, so their code and tests are not implementations or evidence:
  - `rustmath-rings/src/number_field/{morphisms,order,s_unit_solver,tests}.rs`:
    `number_field/mod.rs` declares no submodules.
  - Five modules in `rustmath-calculus` (`laplace`, `maxima_compat`, `minpoly`, `pochhammer`,
    `product`): their `mod` lines are commented out.
  - `rustmath-curves/src/{hyperelliptic,divisor,cantor,jacobian}.rs`: behind the feature `genus2`,
    which is off by default, because they do not compile.
  - `rustmath-combinatorics/src/parking_functions.rs` and
    `rustmath-manifolds/src/tracker_08_09_tests.rs`.
- **Test citations.** Every `done` or `verified` row must name a compiled `#[test]` fn that is not
  `#[ignore]`d, or a doctest.

Sixteen rows failed one of the two checks. They were re-derived, an independent verifier confirmed
every one, and they were committed (`bd98093`).

The orchestrator also spot-checked two or three claims per chapter against the code. Every check
held; the notes are in the session record. Each row's note ends with its provenance:

- `[review 2026-11-06; checked]`: the verifier agreed (3,989 rows).
- `[review 2026-11-06; adjudicated]`: there was a dispute (696 rows).
- `[review 2026-11-06]`: reviewer only. These are 245 `missing` rows outside the verifier's sample.
- `[review 2026-11-06; corrected, ...]`: changed by the correction pass (16 rows).

## What this suggests building first

The core is mostly `missing` or `partial`, and a few L0-L1 bugs sit under everything:

1. **Fix the L0-L1 defects above.** These include polynomial `coeff` and `div_rem` over Z, the
   Legendre symbol, `is_unit`, square roots mod p^k, and the linear-solve and polynomial-SNF bugs.
   Each is small, and each sits under number fields, groups and lattices.
2. **Delete or wire up the dead code.** This covers the `rustmath-rings/src/number_field/` orphan
   files, the gated genus-2 stack in curves, and the commented-out calculus modules.
3. **Then the build targets from `docs/PLAN.md`.** These are GF(p^n) (ch 21 is 20% `done`), general
   Schreier-Sims (ch 58 has `Order` verified for M24 and a BSGS `done`, but no algorithms built on
   it), and the number-field stack. That stack is real but absolute-only: class groups match GP,
   while ray class groups and abelian extensions are broken.
