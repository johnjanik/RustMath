## 2. The criterion and its proof

I checked each claim of Section 2 line by line.

- (F1) and step 1. Correct. The four checks find exactly the factors in one variable: the
  affine gcds give the finite lines, and "no column of full degree" gives `Y0` (or `X0`). Lemma
  9.1's "F mod p has bidegree (a, b)" means `X0` and `Y0` do not divide `F mod p`. Step 1 checks
  exactly that.
- Step 2. Correct. The proof needs only the absence of a factor in `Y` alone for `X`-fibres. A
  factor in `X` alone would already make the fibre reducible. Over infinity the restriction is to
  `Y0 = 0`, and the argument is the same.
- Claim A. Correct. Two small points, neither affecting the result. The orbit product is fixed by
  `sigma` once it is made monic in a fixed monomial order, so Hilbert 90 is not needed. When
  Hilbert 90 is used, note that `N(lambda) = 1` follows from `sigma^n = id`. Multiplicity one uses
  only that `F_p` is perfect, as stated.
- Claim B. Correct (`tau = sigma^d` has `gcd(m, d)` orbits on `Z/m`).
- Claim C. Correct, and the pass is complete over `P^1 x P^1 (F_p)`: affine roots per rational
  fibre (all `x` if a fibre vanishes, which step 1 already excludes), `(inf, y)`, `(x, inf)`,
  `(inf, inf)` through the reversed charts.
- The search never decides from a bounded search. `NotShown` is the only outcome of an empty
  search. Step 1 says "reducible" only with the factor in hand.

Prototype code (`f9/proto/irreducible.rs`) points that soundness depends on, all correct:

- `FormModP` rows are rectangular and never trimmed (`form.rs` `check_shape`), so reversing a
  column and evaluating at 0 gives the coefficient of `Y^dy`. The fibre over infinity is right.
- `factor_degrees` returns `None` when `gcd(a, a') != 1`. When `a' = 0` (a p-th power in `x`),
  `rgcd(a, [])` is `a`, so it is not squarefree and is rejected. The distinct-degree loop uses
  `q = f.order() = p^d`, not `p`.
- `base_points` keeps a `g` only if `factor_with_multiplicity` gives one factor of multiplicity 1
  and degree `d`, so `PolyField` is a field.
- `smooth_rational_point` checks `F(P) = 0` and `(F_X, F_Y)(P) != 0` in the chart. It is called
  only after a fibre has shown irreducibility over `F_p`, as Claim C needs.
- `check_line_components` handles the zero rows through `fp::gcd([0], f) = monic f`.

## 9. Minor issues

- **m1 (F-5 citation).** In Le Lay's `paper/main.tex` at `3f6ab4b`, "Reduction at 23"
  (`\label{sec:reduction}`, line 1047) is Section 4. Section 5 is "Constructing the harmonic
  lift" (`\label{sec:harmonic-construction}`, `paper/harmonic-construction.tex`, input at line
  1313). That section holds the statement the owner relies on. Lemma `lem:marked-distance` and the
  paragraph at `eq:harmonic-good-character` show that the good-reduction field is `K(rho)`,
  `rho^15 = pi`, a tamely ramified extension of degree 15. So "Section 5 'Reduction at 23',
  `sec:reduction`" in F9.md 8.3 and 9 is wrong. Change: cite Section 5 "Constructing the harmonic
  lift" (`sec:harmonic-construction`, Lemma `lem:marked-distance`, the good-reduction field
  `K(rho)`, `rho^15 = pi`), and optionally Section 4 (`sec:reduction`) for the local structure at
  23. Paraphrase only (CC BY-NC-SA).
- **m2 (curve (v): Shape or FibreComponent).** In the certify order of 4.2,
  `check_line_components` (step 3) runs before `absolute_irreducibility` (step 5). For
  `x^2 + 11` of bidegree (2, 0), its x-gcd is `x^2 + 11` of degree 2. So `certify` and
  `normalization_point_counts` return `DegenerateModel(FibreComponent { "x-place g(x) = [11, 0,
  1] (ascending)" })`, not `Err(Shape)` as 2.2 and 8.2 say. (`singular_locus` accepts dy = 0, and
  `p_a = 0` by `saturating_sub`.) 8.1 also lists (v) among the `check_line_components` cases.
  Change: put the zero-entry bidegree check first, in `certify` or in `check_line_components`,
  so the error is `Shape` on every path. Or state that the test calls `absolute_irreducibility`
  directly and that `certify` gives `FibreComponent`.
- **m3 (an untested deciding branch).** No planned test reaches
  `Absolute { Bidegree { gcd >= 2 } }` settled by the residue degree. X at 5 and E6 at 11 use
  gcd 1. This branch can produce a Decided status, so it needs a test. Examples exist
  (`review1/settle2.gp`). At p = 3, the (2,2) form `(y^2 + y + 1) x^2 + (y + 1) x + (2y + 1)` has
  no irreducible rational fibre in either ruling and is settled at degree 2. The (4,2) form
  `(2y^2 + 2) x^4 + (2y^2 + 2y + 1) x^3 + (y^2 + 2y + 1) x^2 + (2y^2 + 2y + 2) x + (2y^2 + 2)` has
  an irreducible rational fibre and no smooth `F_3`-point, and is settled at degree 2. PARI
  confirms both are absolutely irreducible. Change: add one such case to 8.1 or 8.2, with its
  Display string (`... gcd(dx, dy) = 2, settled by the residue degree 2`) and PARI's factorization
  over `F_9` as the reference.
- **m4 (place string of a reducible gcd).** `check_line_components` reports the whole gcd as a
  "y-place". The prototype shows G at 7 as `g(y) = (y - 3)^23`, degree 23, and at 13 a degree-23
  gcd. `place_name` in `singular.rs` uses "place" for an irreducible `g`. Change: report the
  first irreducible factor of the gcd (a true place), or word it "the lines over the roots of
  g(y) = [..]".
- **m5 (search bound in the label and the header).** The `NoIrreducibleFibre` label says "no
  irreducible fibre found over points of degree <= 4". The search covers only the first 64 points
  per ruling and degree, and for p > 63 it never tries infinity. Change: say so in the label, for
  example "(none among the first 64 closed points of each degree <= 4 in either ruling)". The
  prototype's module header also says "at most 256 fibres" while the constant is 64. Fix that
  when the header is written.
- **m6 (L remark).** In 2.5, "ramified ones included" is true but not needed. Every counted L row
  is at an unramified prime, since the ramified primes 2, 11 and 23 have no counted row. Saying
  this in the `calf_mod` doc makes the reliance on the DVR form of the lemma visible only where
  it matters.
- **m7 (nits).** The docstring of `l_covers.py` says `7 <= p <= 127`, but the code runs from 5.
  Section 4.1 cites ch. 24 for factorization over `F_q`. The fibre tests are univariate (ch. 23,
  `Chapter_23_Univariate_Polynomial_Rings.md`), and the bivariate reference is PARI. Optional
  hardening: make `IrreducibilityEvidence` and the `Certificate` variants
  `#[non_exhaustive]`, or give them private fields, so that only `certify` can build a Decided
  status.
