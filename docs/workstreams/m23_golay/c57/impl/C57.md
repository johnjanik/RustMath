## 0. Summary

  `F_2[x]`-module from `P` alone: the types allowed by S1, S2 (the Weil pairing) and by the rank
  bounds `dim ker(F^k -+ 1) <= v_2(P^(k)(+-1))` for every `k | N_max`, decided only when every

### FR1.4 For the owner: refused L rows (m5)

RustMath writes its own `reason` on a refused row. On the four refused L rows it is "model
degenerate at this prime: the discriminant vanishes identically mod p". `zeta_L.json` has the same
text at (11, 2), and other texts at (5, 3) ("the degree in V drops mod p"), (23, 5) and (23, 15)
("the characteristic divides the degree in V"). RustMath raises this refusal only after it has
computed the formal discriminant of `F` as a binary form in `X` (the fibre variable of degree 4)
and found it identically zero mod p (`nodal_biprojective/singular.rs:427-432`). So the text is
an accurate statement of what RustMath tested. A binary-form discriminant vanishes identically
when every fibre has a multiple root, the root at `X = infinity` included, so a drop of the
`X`-degree by 2 or more also gives it. The owner's texts name a test in his fibration in `V`.
Whether the two tests have the same cause at these three rows would need the `calF` model and
F-9 code; that is out of scope here. Both refuse; neither gives a count. A script that compares
the two files should compare `status`, not `reason`. The counted L rows also carry
`certificate.certified` (true when the row is Decided), which the owner's L rows lack; it is an
extra key.
